# 🏎️ Furious 7 (Fast & Furious 7)

> *"I don't have friends. I got family."* — **Dominic Toretto**

## 📖 Overview
**Furious 7** (2015) is an action-packed thriller directed by *James Wan* and written by *Chris Morgan*. It is the seventh installment in the **Fast & Furious** franchise and follows Dominic Toretto, Brian O'Conner, and their crew as they face off against a rogue special forces assassin, **Deckard Shaw**, who is seeking revenge for his comatose brother.
